The domain bar's default look is now `toolbox`: one low line glued to the top edge of the content, full width, a rule at the bottom, drawn like the side menu (its sizes, square entries, the current one tinted and underlined). `look: 'card'` keeps the card. The `ribbon` look of 2.55 is withdrawn: it draws the toolbox, with a warning in development; its `--qd-ribbon-*` properties are gone, the toolbox's are `--qd-toolbox-*`.
