A domain declares `sources`, reads its indicators share: an indicator names one with `source` and takes its part with `value(data)`, in place of its own `load()`. One read per `refresh` whatever the number of indicators, its error on each, the answer reused while fresh when the bar comes back; `entities` names what it is derived from, and a change to one of them (a local write, the live stream, `invalidate()`) reads it again, coalesced. An unknown source is warned and marks the indicator (#5461)
