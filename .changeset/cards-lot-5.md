Eight more generic cards (#5355, lot 5 of #5337), each with its sheet, schema and example, in the index, the bestiary and the gallery: `compare` (two values and the change, in points or percent), `trend` (a curve with its band of normal, the points outside marked and counted), `ranking` (the first N, with optional bars), `split` (a total, one stacked bar with a fixed palette by rank and "others", each part written), `funnel` (steps scaled to the first, the loss between steps in words), `heat` (a grid on a five-step sequential scale, each cell's value in its title), `agenda` (what is coming, a time today, "tomorrow", a date) and `feed` (what happened, the latest first, a status as a mark and a word).
