Favourites have one symbol, the bookmark (filled once a favourite), icon alone with its words in aria-label and tooltip, for a page and for a search; a list's search bookmark sits in its filter zone, and a click on a saved one takes it out again. The look is the app's: `features.favorites` (`icon`, `iconActive`, `saveSearch: 'filters' | 'header' | false`, `labels`), each use overriding it by props (`icon`, `iconOn`, `labelled`, ListPage's `save-search`, QdFavoritesCard's `icon`).
